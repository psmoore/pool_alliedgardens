About the pool

Main pool

  • 25 yards long by 25 meters wide
  • 3.5 to 12 feet deep
  • 11 lanes
  • 1-meter diving board

Wading pool

  • 2 feet deep

Amenities: restrooms; disabled access with ramp and lift; drinking fountain; outdoor heated showers; picnic tables; lockers (bring your own lock); outdoor lighting; indoor changing stalls; power outlets; bleachers with a shaded spectator area. No smoking, no alcohol.

What this replaces

  • A PDF in last year's folder. The "Fall Program Guide" link opens /sites/default/files/2024-04/alliedgardenspoolprogram.pdf, a file in an April 2024 folder that now holds the fall 2026 schedule. Each season the City re-makes and re-posts it. Here the staff change the schedule once and the page follows.
  • A closure notice from last spring. The page still says "Allied Garden Pool will be closed for maintenance from May 20th to May 27th". Here a closure shows only until it's over, and the next long maintenance closure is already on the calendar.
  • Flyers that disagree with the schedule. The City's free intro water polo flyer says Allied Gardens runs it "Fri/Sat 3:00-4:00pm", and the pool's own fall program sheet disagrees with it. Here there is one schedule for flyers and pages to read from.
  • "Limited lap lanes during some scheduled programs." Lap swimmers can't tell how many lanes are left when classes and teams are in the water. Here every program holds its lanes, so the lane-by-lane view shows what's open.
  • Sixteen pools, sixteen PDFs. Every City of San Diego pool has this same page and its own program sheet. The same calendar and page format could run all of them, with one citywide view of every pool's lap swim.
